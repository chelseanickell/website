Home of Chelsea Nickell

You've found my corner of the internet. This website will act as a personal blog, repository of project documentation and generally whatever I feel like putting on it. I'm an IT professional and overrall nerd and I'm constantly learning new technology and trying out new things. You can expect a lot about Linux, especially NixOS and Gentoo.

The word "syzygy" comes from the Ancient Greek suzugía (συζυγία) meaning a "union", "yoke", or "pair". It's used in a multitude of different contexts including:

  • Astronomy — As an alignment of three or more celestial bodies in a straight line
  • Mathematics — A linear relation of a generating set to a finitely generated module
  • Biology — The pairing of chromosomes during cellular division meiosis
  • Philosophy — Generally to mean the pairing of opposites. For me, this is most notable in Carl Jung's work as the pairing of Anima and Animus.
